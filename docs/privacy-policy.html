Matofali: Brick Breaker

Ilisasishwa / Last updated: 27 October 2026

Sera ya faragha

Matofali haikusanyi, haihifadhi kwenye seva, wala haitumi taarifa zako binafsi. Mchezo unahifadhi kwenye simu yako tu alama yako bora, ngazi ya juu uliyofikia, na mipangilio ya sauti, mtetemo na lugha. Hakuna matangazo, akaunti wala ufuatiliaji. Mchezo hautumii mtandao. Ukifuta programu, taarifa hizi zinafutwa pia.

Privacy policy

Matofali does not collect, upload or share any personal information. The game stores only your best score, the highest level you reached, and your sound, vibration and language settings, and it keeps them on your phone. There are no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking, and the game does not use the internet. Uninstalling the app deletes this information.

The app asks for no special permissions. Android lists "full network access", which the app's built-in web view needs to display the game; the game sends nothing over the network. Vibration is used only when you lose a life and can be turned off in Settings.
