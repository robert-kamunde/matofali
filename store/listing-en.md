**App name (30 max):** Matofali: Brick Breaker

**Short description (80 max):** Break the bricks! A Tanzanian brick game with Kilimanjaro, Zanzibar and more.

**Full description:**

Matofali is a brick-breaker game made in Tanzania. Drag your finger to move the flag-coloured paddle, tap to launch the ball, and break every brick to reach the next level.

Every level is a picture made of bricks:
• Bendera – the national flag
• Kilimanjaro – the Roof of Africa, with its snow cap
• Mlango wa Zanzibar – a carved Zanzibar door
• Serengeti – the endless plains under a yellow sun

Clear all four and they come back faster and tougher, so there is always a higher score to chase.

Catch falling power-ups:
↔ Wide paddle for 12 seconds
×3 Three balls at once
♥ An extra life

• Plays completely offline, no internet or data needed
